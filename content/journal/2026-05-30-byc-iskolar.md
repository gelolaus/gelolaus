When I found out I was selected as one of the first scholars under the iSkolar platform supported by BYC Ventures, I couldn't believe it. Like many Filipino students, struggling to afford the extra costs of specialized software and digital tools can be an uphill battle each semester. Receiving this ₱2,500.00 scholarship means more than financial help, it means someone believes in my potential.

This scholarship will go a long way for me. It takes care of my monthly load allowance so I can stay online without interruptions during heavy research and coding sessions when off-site. The rest will go toward digital school requirements like cloud storage and the AI tools I use for my workflows, which usually take up a huge chunk of my student budget. Huge thanks to iSkolar and BYC Ventures for being the first to trust scholars like me. I will definitely make this count.

Honestly, I wasn't expecting the process to be this fast. I have friends who have waited months for their scholarships to come in, and some even had to borrow money just to get by while waiting. With iSkolar, the application was easy to navigate, I always knew exactly what was happening with my status, and it felt like a legit, professional process from start to finish. The moment it hit me that this was real was when the disbursement came through quickly right when I needed it, without me having to chase anyone or follow up endlessly. It feels completely different, like the system was actually built with students in mind. I wish all scholarships were this efficient. iSkolar is definitely changing the game, and I am proud to be one of the first scholars on the platform.
